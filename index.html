Fit isn't one size. It's yours.

This is the desk Sewnie is built around: measuring tape, cutting mat, machine, and a stack of ideas pinned to the wall. Scroll to walk through what each corner of it means for the app.

01. The dress form

Start with your own measurements

No size chart, no guesswork. You (or a form built to your shape) get measured once, and every pattern and alteration Sewnie generates after that is drafted against the body that's actually wearing the clothes.

02. The cutting table

Or draft it from scratch

Enter your measurements and Sewnie drafts a sewing pattern from the ground up: bodice, sleeve, waist, all sized to you. Export it and print it at true scale, ready to lay on fabric.

03. The sewing machine

Fix what you already own

Photograph a piece that doesn't fit and tell Sewnie what's off. It reads the construction and builds an alteration plan: the exact seams to open, how much to take in, and the order to do it in.

04. The moodboard

Thrift finds, reimagined

Point your camera at anything on the rack. Sewnie sketches out a few ways it could become something else, so you know what you're buying before you buy it.
